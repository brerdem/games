FLOW FORT

Send the right color before the horde reaches the wall.

How to play

Goal. Clear every colored block before the horde marches into the fort wall.

Gunners. Tap the front gunner in a column to send it clockwise around the rail. It shoots only the first block in a lane, and only when that block matches its color. Purple gunners never match a block: they only fill the bench. Keys 1–4 send columns. Q W E R T send the five bench slots. R still launches a bench slot.

Bench. Leftover ammo waits in a slot. Five slots. A sixth leftover jams and costs a heart. Restart is the on-screen Restart button or Backspace, not R.

The horde. Blocks step toward the wall. A row that hits the wall costs a heart and is thrown back to the top, so letting it through never clears the level. Calm mode, on the title screen, stops the march. Esc pauses. M mutes.

Score. 10 points for each block shot, plus a bonus for hearts and empty bench slots. Three stars means a clear with every heart and no jam. Calm caps stars at two.
