FLOW FORT

How to play

Goal. Clear every colored block before the horde marches into the fort wall.

Gunners. Tap the front gunner in a column to send it clockwise around the rail. It shoots only the first block in a lane, and only when that block matches its color. The number on it is how many shots it has. Arrow keys pick a gunner. Enter or Space sends it. Keys 1–3 send the columns.

Bench. If it still has shots after a full lap, it waits in a slot above the deck. Tap it, or press Q W E R T, to send it again. Five slots. A sixth leftover jams and costs a heart. Only four gunners can ride the rail at once.

The horde. The blocks step down on a timer. A row that hits the wall is destroyed and costs a heart. You also lose if you run out of gunners with blocks still standing.

Score. 10 points for each block shot, plus a bonus for hearts and empty bench slots when the level clears.
