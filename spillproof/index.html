First Pour

Spillproof

Pour each tank to the line the gauges allow. A pipe shares one waterline.

How to play

Tap a cell to preview the waterline, then tap it again to pour. A wrong pour spills a drop and frosts that row and everything above it. Long-press, or the Mark button, sets a dry mark. Those are free. A duck sits on the surface.

Retry?

The board, the drops, and the marks all reset.
