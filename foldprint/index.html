How to fold

Drag a crease tab across the dashed line. Paint on one side prints onto the other. Both sides keep every colour that touched.

Red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green, red and blue make purple. Paint never wipes off. A crease can be pressed again.

A print that lands off the pencil stencil is a smudge: the sheet springs back, and the press is still spent. Undo costs a press too.
