SCRATCH & SLAY

How to play

Goal. Clear three fights. The last one is a lich. Scratch a ticket, then cash in swords, shields, hearts, and coins before you uncover too many skulls.

Scratch. Drag across the silver coat, or tap a cell to clear it. Arrow keys move the cursor and Space clears that cell. A cell opens once about half of its coat is gone.

Skulls. The ticket tells you how many skulls are hidden. The third skull breaks the ticket: nothing you uncovered counts, and the enemy's next attack hits harder.

Cash In. Enter or the button resolves every open symbol. Three matching symbols in a line are doubled once. A star doubles your biggest open number. Shields block the enemy's hit, then fade. Enemy block soaks your swords.

Fights. The foe's next move is written above it. After a win, pick one reward. Steady Hand keeps your first scratch off a skull. Bone Collector makes an open skull nick the foe when you cash in.
