How to knock

Swipe a standing domino along its length. It lies down in the next square, and that square is taken.

A domino in the way falls too, if it has room. Hit one from the side and it spins, still standing. Corners turn the chain. Splitters send it both ways.

Undo costs a push. Restart is free. Clear the table in par pushes for three stars.

Restart?

The table stands back up. Pushes come back. A hint you already used stays used. This is free.
