SMASH CASCADE

How to play

Goal. Knock every crown off the shelf. Glass, wood, and stone hold the tower up. Anything that loses its support falls.

Cannon. Pull back from the cannon and release to fire. The dotted line shows the start of the shot. Left and right aim, up and down set power, and Space or Enter fires.

Cascade. Blocks and the cannonball fall through the pegs into the buckets. A bucket multiplies the coins. An orange bucket gives the ball back. You only get two block refunds per level. The cannonball can always come back.

Levels. The first tower is glass. The next adds wood and stone. The last one hides a red charge that pops nearby blocks.

Score. Coins from the buckets, plus 20 for each ball you have left when the crowns are down. Run out of balls with a crown still on the shelf and the run ends.
