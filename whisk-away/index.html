How to whisk

Drag a cloth slowly along its tassel and everything on it comes along.

Flick the tassel, or double-tap the cloth, and it vanishes. The dishes stay, and drop onto the cloth below.

A cloth cannot sweep through a dish that is standing on a lower layer. Undo −1 spends one move. Restart puts the budget back.
