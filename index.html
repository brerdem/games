Games

Short puzzle games. Every one starts with a button, and you can play with the mouse or the arrow keys.

Unravel

Eat a pixel picture with colored spools. A spool only reaches the bottom pixel of a column. Click a spool, or pick one with the arrow keys and press Enter.

Pinball Pit

Aim colored balls into blocks and clear the pit. Surprise blocks can hand you a card. Drag to aim, or use the arrow keys, then release or press Space.

Gridlock

Slide every arrow car out through a matching exit before hearts, moves, or the clock run out. Click a car, or select it with the arrow keys and press Enter.

Ring Out

Turn the rings until a gap lines up, then fire each pin outward. A shot kicks its ring, so the order you clear them matters.

Smash Cascade

Cannon the crowns off the tower and let the pieces fall through pegs into buckets. Pull back to fire, or aim with the arrow keys and press Space.

Scratch & Slay

Scratch a ticket for swords and shields, then cash in before the third skull. Drag to scratch, or move with the arrow keys and press Space. Three fights, then a lich.

Flow Fort

Send color-matched gunners around the fort. Each one only shoots its own color, so the order matters. Tap a gunner, or choose with the arrow keys and press Enter.

Sand Strata

Pour colored sand into a bottle to match the picture, from the bottom layer up. Slide the hopper and hold to pour, or move with the arrow keys and hold Space.

Top Turner

Carve a spinning top, then launch it into the bowl. The shape sets its weight and spin. Drag to shave, pull back to launch, and hold a direction to dash.
