SAND STRATA

How to play

Goal. Rebuild the sand picture in the bottle, from the bottom layer up. Gravity keeps every grain where it lands.

Pour. Slide the hopper over the mouth and hold to pour. Move while you pour to spread a layer, or hold still to pile a dune. Click a color, or press 1–4. Arrow keys move the hopper. Hold Space to pour.

Match. The little bottle is the target. Colored ticks on the left mark the layer changes. Press G to ghost the target over your sand. Each color runs out when its bar is empty.

Seal. Press Seal or Enter once the sand has settled. 75% match clears the bottle. A closer match scores more. Below 75% you try the bottle again.
