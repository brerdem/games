RING OUT

How to play

Goal. Spin the rings so a gap lines up, then fire every pin out of the circle.

Rings. Drag a ring around the center. It snaps to a step, and each step costs one move. Up and down (or W and S) pick a ring. Left and right (or A and D) rotate it.

Pins. Tap a pin to launch it outward. Space or Enter launches the highlighted pin. Tab changes which pin is highlighted. Launching is free. A pin escapes only when every ring outside it has a gap on that line. A pin sitting on an outer ring blocks that line too.

Colors. The last level adds colored gates. A gate only lets a pin of the same color through. A plain gap lets any color through. White pins cannot use a colored gate. Launching a pin does not cut a new hole in its ring.

Hearts and moves. A blocked launch costs a heart. You start with 3. You also lose if you spend the last move while no pin has a clear path. If one is already lined up, you can still launch it.

Score. 100 for each pin that escapes, plus a combo when you launch several without rotating, and a bonus for moves and hearts left.
